Methodology

Every number in this report is computed deterministically from the monthly billing exports (and, where enabled, historical weather); nothing is generated by a language model, and no conclusion appears without traceable evidence.

  • Seasonality and trend: STL decomposition of the monthly series, with seasonal/trend strength measured by the standard Hyndman–Athanasopoulos statistics.
  • Weather adjustment: a degree-day "energy signature" regression (average daily kWh vs. heating/cooling degree days, bases 15.5 °C/22 °C), the standard utility-analyst method for monthly billing data. Weather comes from the free Open-Meteo archive for the configured location.
  • Usage shifts: PELT and CUSUM change-point detection on the deseasonalized series, so regular winter/summer swings aren't mistaken for behavioural change.
  • Forecast: eight models (naive, seasonal-naive, linear trend, Holt-Winters, SARIMA, Prophet, XGBoost, LightGBM) compared by 1-step-ahead walk-forward cross-validation; the lowest-error model is selected, and uncertainty bands come from resampling that model's own validation errors (floored at zero).
  • Unusual months: three independent detectors (rolling z-score with a shifted baseline, STL-residual generalized ESD, Isolation Forest), cross-referenced -- months flagged by two or more methods are treated as meaningfully more reliable.
  • Cost: effective £/kWh derived directly from the billing exports. Standing charges are not present in the source data and are therefore never estimated -- all £ figures are energy consumption cost only.
  • Carbon and benchmarks: static, cited constants (UK Government DESNZ/DEFRA greenhouse-gas conversion factors; Ofgem Typical Domestic Consumption Values) -- documented in the application's configuration, not live data.
